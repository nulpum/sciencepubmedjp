Traditional Y-chromosome markers trace male lineage but struggle to differentiate fathers from sons or brothers. Rapidly mutating (RM) Y-STR markers solve this limitation with higher mutation rates that create detectable differences between generations. These markers show promise in forensic casework for analyzing mixed DNA samples with multiple male contributors and identifying disaster victims. Beyond crime labs, RM Y-STRs may aid clinical investigations of unexplained infertility and reproductive risks. Advanced techniques like massively parallel sequencing (MPS) have enhanced their utility, though challenges remain: mutation rates vary across populations, and standardized interpretation methods are still developing. Expanding databases like the Y chromosome Haplotype Reference Database (YHRD) to include these markers will be crucial for realizing their full potential in forensics and beyond.
