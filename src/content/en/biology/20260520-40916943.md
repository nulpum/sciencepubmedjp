Researchers analysed literature from PubMed, CNKI, and Web of Science to assess the state of TCM decoction preparation facilities. Their review identified that equipment and facilities are often inadequate, staff processes lack standardisation, and management systems have significant flaws. To address these challenges, the authors propose four key improvements: establishing safer, more hygienic environments; upgrading equipment and facilities; strengthening staff training to improve quality; and enhancing overall management practices. These findings highlight the need for better quality control measures in TCM preparation facilities, though the review focuses on identifying problems and proposing standards rather than evaluating clinical outcomes of the decoctions themselves.
