Researchers isolated 100 actinomycetes strains from Tunisian environments ranging from oases and rhizosphere soils to saline wetlands and ancient monument rocks. Phylogenetic analysis showed most belonged to the *Streptomyces* genus. Among the 33 natural compounds extracted, one molecule called 3-O-methylviridicatin showed antitumor activity in laboratory tests and was associated with suppressed HIV expression. The study highlights how Tunisia's geographic diversity—spanning coastal zones, desert deposits, and even offshore oil rigs—creates unique microbial ecosystems. These findings demonstrate the country's potential as a source for natural product discovery, with actinomycetes proving particularly productive in varied ecological niches.
