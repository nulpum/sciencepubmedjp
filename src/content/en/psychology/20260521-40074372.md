A scoping review of 19 studies examined asynchronous active learning activities in nursing education between 2016 and 2023. Researchers found that methods like game-based learning, interactive case studies, video discussions, and virtual simulations were linked to improved student engagement and critical thinking. While discussion boards remain popular in online nursing courses, they often lack meaningful interaction. The review identified varied evaluation approaches, including surveys, course grades, and program outcomes. These findings suggest that diversifying online learning strategies may help nursing faculty enhance both student satisfaction and academic performance in asynchronous courses, though traditional discussion boards continue to dominate current practice.
