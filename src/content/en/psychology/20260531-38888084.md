Following the success of writing accountability groups in academia, scientists introduced TAGs (Technology Accountability Groups) as a structured support system for graduate students and faculty in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics fields. These groups aim to help members navigate technological innovations, learn new skills, and connect with field leaders. The framework addresses a growing challenge in STEM: as technology develops at breakneck speed, academics face increasing pressure to master new tools and applications. TAGs provide community support, motivation, and skill-building opportunities to reduce this technology-related stress while fostering professional connections across disciplines.
