Researchers are using big data to uncover the collective dynamics behind success in science, technology, business, and the arts. This cross-disciplinary work has identified common patterns that can even predict success across different domains. However, these studies also reveal concerning biases that contradict meritocratic ideals—suggesting that talent and hard work aren't the only factors determining who rises to the top. The findings have implications for hiring decisions, funding allocations, and career planning. Scientists are now calling for more research into how cultural factors and algorithms may perpetuate inequalities, with the goal of aligning success measures more closely with societal values rather than perpetuating existing disparities.
