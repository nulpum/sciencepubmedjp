New clinical guidelines recommend a combined medication regimen of mifepristone followed by misoprostol for managing early pregnancy loss (EPL), showing stronger evidence than misoprostol alone. The recommended protocol involves mifepristone 200 mg orally, followed 7-48 hours later by misoprostol 800 mcg. When mifepristone is unavailable, guidelines suggest two or more doses of misoprostol at 600-800 mcg intervals. The recommendations emphasize patient-centered care through shared decision-making, allowing expectant management for up to 8 weeks when medically appropriate. Notably, guidelines suggest against routine Rh testing before 12 weeks' gestation for medication management, and recommend against using endometrial thickness alone to determine if additional intervention is needed. Ibuprofen 800 mg is suggested for pain control during the process.
