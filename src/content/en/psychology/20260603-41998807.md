A panel of dermatologists conducted a systematic review and developed five consensus statements on the link between high-curl-pattern hair and scalp barrier health. The structural characteristics of tightly coiled hair increase vulnerability to breakage and to seborrheic dermatitis (SD), an inflammatory scalp condition. Certain hair care practices common in this population may contribute to or worsen SD, which can cause significant psychological distress. The experts noted that ceramide-containing products are associated with improvements in the condition. However, they emphasized that further research is needed to understand racial and ethnic variations in SD and to support culturally sensitive, individualized treatment approaches for managing this scalp condition in people with high-curl-pattern hair.
