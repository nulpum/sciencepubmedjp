Despite being the most prevalent sleep disorder, insomnia in young people frequently goes untreated. This gap in care is particularly concerning because adequate sleep plays a crucial role in proper brain development. Children and adolescents may be especially vulnerable to the negative effects associated with chronic sleep difficulties. A 2024 comprehensive review emphasizes that proper clinical assessment and treatment of pediatric insomnia is essential, noting that the disorder presents differently across various stages of child development. The findings highlight a significant mismatch between how common the condition is and how often it receives medical attention in younger populations.
