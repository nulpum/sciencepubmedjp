An integrative review of 11 studies explored virtual reality-reminiscence intervention (VR-RI), a non-pharmacological approach that immerses patients in memory-triggering virtual environments. The synthesis revealed that VR-RI is generally feasible and acceptable for older adults with dementia or cognitive impairment, representing a potential tool to support mental health in this population.

However, researchers caution that the evidence remains limited. Studies suffered from small sample sizes, varied designs, and inconsistent approaches to reminiscence therapy. Key factors like dementia type, whether content was familiar to participants, and personalisation based on individual past experiences were often overlooked. While early findings are encouraging, more rigorous research is needed to determine optimal implementation strategies and which patients may benefit most from this emerging intervention approach.
